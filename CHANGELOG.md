# Changelog

## 1.5.3

- Centered the wind gust reading in the header instrument, matching the dew point and pressure captions.


## 1.5.2

- Removed the rotating arrow beside the wind direction in the header; the compass direction remains visible.


## 1.5.1

- Simplified the header summary to icons and values, removing the repeated labels.
- Increased summary text and icon sizes while keeping the metrics on one row on narrow screens.


## 1.5.0

- Renamed the card to arn0ld2000 SMHI Card and updated its HACS repository/resource/type names.
- Added support for one to three user-configured SMHI locations, each with its own entity and coordinates.
- Made the displayed provider consistently SMHI.

## 1.4.8

- Weather artwork is distinct across clear, cloudy, fog, windy, snow, mixed precipitation, hail, and thunder conditions. Rain has six intensity levels.
- Enlarged the header weather illustration.
- Wind direction and gusts are larger and shown side by side. Humidity and dew point are clearer. Pressure shows a low/normal/high label and difference from standard pressure (not a time trend).
